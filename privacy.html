Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Study Planner is a planner app for Mac made by a student. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Without an account

If you don't sign in, everything you add (tasks, subjects, dates, your name and profile photo) is saved only on your own Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With an account

If you create an account or sign in, the app stores:

  • Your email address, so you can sign in.
  • Your planner (tasks, subjects, dates, notes, your name and profile photo), so it's backed up and can be used on another Mac.

This is stored with Supabase, the service that runs the accounts. Each person can only access their own planner. Your password is never stored by the app.

Sign in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile (name and picture) with the app. This is only used to create and sign in to your account. The app doesn't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no tracking.
  • Your information is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete your tasks in the app at any time (Settings → Data). To delete your whole account and everything stored with it, email the address below and it will be removed.
